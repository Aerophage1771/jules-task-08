Practice & Explanations

PT 89, Section 2, Question 14

Detailed Explanation

Gap Analysis: The argument jumps from a correlation between early music exposure and cognitive development to a causal claim that music lessons cause the development.

(A) Correct: Points out an alternative cause (parents who enroll kids in music may also engage in other developmental activities).

(B) Incorrect: Fails to address the causal gap; merely discusses a subset of children.

Blind Review & Wrong Answer Journal

A review is useful only if it changes a future decision.

5-Step Method: 1. Job → 2. Claims → 3. Gap → 4. Prephrase → 5. Verify
Identifier Analysis (Where it broke) Correction Rule for the Future
PT 92, S3, Q12
Missed		 Misidentified the conclusion; chose intermediate conclusion. The final sentence was actually a premise supporting the first sentence. Always test claims with the "Why?" bridge. If A supports B, B is the conclusion.
PT 91, S1, Q08
Guessed		 Fell for a scope shift trap in an inference question. Choice C went beyond the text by asserting 'all' instead of 'most'. Inference answers must be 100% provable. Beware strong quantifiers.
